        FAQ

        Hiring an e-commerce SEO specialist in the Philippines

        What does an e-commerce SEO specialist do?

        An e-commerce SEO specialist helps an online store get found on Google by shoppers who are ready to buy. That means keyword research around buying intent, optimizing collection and product pages, writing blog content that answers pre-purchase questions, fixing internal links and metadata, and tracking results in Google Search Console.

        Why hire an e-commerce SEO specialist from the Philippines?

        You get experienced, English-first SEO work at a lower cost than most US, UK or Australian agencies. Working from Cebu, I overlap with Asia-Pacific business hours and can deliver overnight for US and European teams. My work has ranked #1 on Google for international brands like Keychron and has been featured by Backlinko.

        Which e-commerce platforms do you work with?

        I mainly work with Shopify and WooCommerce (WordPress) stores. I handle collection pages, product pages, blog articles, metadata and internal linking, and I use tools like Ahrefs, Semrush, Screaming Frog, RankMath and Google Search Console.

        How long does e-commerce SEO take to show results?

        Most stores see early movement in impressions within 4 to 8 weeks and stronger ranking and traffic gains within 3 to 6 months, depending on competition and site authority. For one baby-care store, new pages I wrote reached more than 50,000 impressions in two months.

        Can you get my store's content into Google AI Overviews?

        No one can guarantee an AI Overview placement, but content built around clear, direct answers to buyer questions has the best chance. My articles for Keychron were cited in Google's AI Overview and People Also Ask for searches like "red switch vs brown switch".
